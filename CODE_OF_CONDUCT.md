# Code of Conduct

OpenMargam is for practical mentorship and professional guidance.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Avoid harassment, spam, impersonation, and credential misrepresentation.
- Avoid guaranteed outcome claims for jobs, admissions, funding, investment returns, immigration outcomes, or similar high-risk promises.
- Respect privacy and do not pressure users to share sensitive personal, financial, or account information.
- Use public places and explicit consent for in-person meetings.

Maintainers may remove content, suspend accounts, or restrict participation when behavior creates safety, fraud, privacy, or trust risks.
